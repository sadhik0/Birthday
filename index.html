Happy Birthday, My Dear Reshu ❤️

My dearest Reshu,

If I could give you one thing today, it would be the chance to see yourself through my eyes, even for just a moment. Maybe then you'd understand how special you are and how much happiness your presence can bring into someone's life.

Today is all about celebrating you — your beautiful heart, your smile, the little things that make you uniquely you, and all the wonderful things that make you the person you are.

I hope this new chapter brings you countless reasons to smile, dreams that slowly become reality, opportunities that make you proud, and people who remind you how deeply you are valued. On the difficult days, I hope you find the strength to keep going and the comfort of knowing that brighter days are still ahead.

And selfishly, I hope I get to be part of many of those beautiful days — sharing silly moments, making memories, laughing at things nobody else would understand, and finding happiness in the little things together.

You deserve a year filled with peace in your heart, warmth in your days, courage in your dreams, and a kind of happiness that stays long after the celebrations are over.

So here's to another chapter of your beautiful story, Reshu. May it be kinder, brighter, more exciting, and even more wonderful than you imagined.

Happy birthday, my dear. Keep smiling, keep dreaming, and never forget how special you are.

With all my love, always yours. ❤️

For Reshu · 6 November
